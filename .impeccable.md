## Design Context

### Users
Music artists, VTubers, creators, and corporate clients who are considering MV, cover MV, lyric video, teaser, or short-form video production. They need to judge visual quality, production scale, budget fit, and whether contacting Riesz feels low-risk.

### Brand Personality
Precise, cinematic, and trusted. The site should feel like a focused production portfolio rather than a marketing landing page.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, restrained, editorial interface with bright green accents, strong work imagery, and direct contact paths. Avoid decorative gradients, loud effects, generic SaaS patterns, and overly cute presentation.

### Design Principles
- Let the work carry trust; keep UI copy concise and practical.
- Make the path from a reference work to contact short and obvious.
- Keep pricing and production-scale language clear without implying fixed estimates.
- Preserve a high-end, quiet tone with dense but readable information.
- Prioritize mobile touch targets, accessible forms, and stable layouts.
