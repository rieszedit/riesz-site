# Hybrid Standard Positioning Update

## Goal

Position Hybrid Standard as a value-adding specialist collaboration rather than a lower-cost outsourcing option, while preserving a clear reason to choose Riesz Main Standard.

## Approved Direction

- Raise Hybrid Standard from JPY 130,000 to JPY 170,000 before tax.
- Keep the plan name `Hybrid Standard`.
- Describe Riesz as responsible for the movie, direction, and finishing.
- Describe the collaborator as strengthening lyric design and text direction.
- Limit the quality claim to lyric and text expression. Do not claim that Hybrid is universally higher quality than Riesz Main.
- Keep Riesz Main Standard at JPY 150,000 and leave its scope unchanged.

## Copy

Japanese copy must explain that specialist lyric-design participation improves the completeness of lyric and text expression through a controlled division of responsibilities. Remove wording that frames Hybrid Standard as a reduced production scope.

English copy must communicate the same value and responsibility split without presenting collaboration as a discount route.
